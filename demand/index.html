interesting is cheap

A product idea earns a build only when the stakes are real, the generic model fails, and the obvious labs are not already hunting the individual segment. All three. No exceptions.

This is a decision filter, not a trend report. The examples below show the method. The form makes you use it.

01

The stakes are real

The buyer loses money, a client, a round, or a reputation if they get this wrong. Curiosity is not a budget.

Pass test: name the person, the loss, and the moment the loss becomes visible.

02

The generic model fails

The useful answer needs private context, a repeatable workflow, or judgment that a three-line prompt cannot supply.

Pass test: explain what a generic chat answer gets wrong and what your product must know.

03

The individual segment is open

Large labs build for enterprise verticals. Your buyer is the individual layer they do not need to own.

Pass test: name the buyer and show why a lab's enterprise product does not serve them.
